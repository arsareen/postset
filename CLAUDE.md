# CLAUDE.md

## What this is
PostSet, a shared clip library for festival and concert crowds. Attendees upload the songs they filmed, each clip manually tagged to its track, and anyone can open a song and watch every uploaded angle. Capstone for IST300.

## Who uses it
A festival and show regular in their early twenties, on their phone. They film a few favorite songs, want to stop filming the rest so they can stay present, and later come back as a viewer to watch the songs they missed from multiple angles.

## Scope
- Must: upload a clip, manually tag it to a song, browse by song, watch multiple angles, plus accounts.
- Should: search by song and event.
- Later, not now: add a friend and any social layer, Wrapped style stats, precise in set timestamp positioning, auto song detection.

## Conventions
- Requirements will live in docs/02-prd.md once it exists. If a request contradicts it, say so rather than guessing.
