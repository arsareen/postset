# PostSet: Concept Brief

## 1. Working name
PostSet

## 2. The pitch, in one sentence
PostSet is a shared clip library for festival and concert crowds where everyone uploads the songs they filmed and tags each clip to the track it belongs to, so anyone can open a song and watch it from every angle in the crowd, even the songs they never recorded themselves.

## 3. Who it's for, specifically
Honestly, me first. I go to a lot of concerts and festivals and find myself always being stuck on my phone instead of trying to live in the moment. A festival and show regular in their early twenties, on their phone, who films a few of their favorite songs and wants to stop filming the rest so they can actually be present. Later that same person opens PostSet as a viewer, wanting to watch the tracks they missed, or only caught from one bad spot in the back.

## 4. The job it does
When I'm at a show and I can't record every song without missing the experience, I want to pool my clips with everyone else's and tag them by song, so I can stay in the moment and still watch any track later from every angle in the crowd.

## 5. What people do instead today
People try to film everything themselves and still miss songs, then afterward dig through scattered Instagram stories, random YouTube uploads, and TikTok hashtags, hoping a stranger happened to post the exact song from a decent angle. Also, people don't live in the moment anymore and simply worry about always recording. There is no place where clips are organized by song, or where you can line up several angles of the same track, so most people settle for their own shaky single angle clip, or nothing.

## 6. Your biggest unknown
Whether enough people at the same show will actually upload to make the coverage real. The whole promise, watch any song from any angle, only holds if a crowd contributes rather than a handful of people, so the cold start problem is the core risk. Right behind it is whether people will share into a shared pool at all, instead of keeping clips to themselves.

## Optional: a stack guess
Today it is a Python build script generating a static site in a GitHub repo that auto deploys to Vercel. That will change quickly once real uploads, storage, accounts, and search are involved, probably a proper backend with a video pipeline and authentication. Week 7 is where the stack actually gets decided, so this is just today's guess.
